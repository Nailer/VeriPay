## How Refunds Work

VeriPay uses a **two-step, consent-based refund mechanism**. This means the buyer cannot unilaterally withdraw funds — the seller must first agree to the refund. This prevents fraud from both sides.

### The Refund Flow

```
Buyer wants refund → Seller approves refund → Buyer claims refund
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1: Seller Authorizes Refund">
    **Who:** The seller (the wallet that was designated to receive funds)

    **How:** On the trade detail page (`/trade/{id}`), the seller clicks **"Authorize Return of Funds"**.

    **What happens on-chain:**
    ```
    sellerApproveRefund(tradeId)
    ├── Verifies caller is the seller
    ├── Sets trade.sellerApprovedRefund = true
    └── Emits RefundAuthorized(id) event
    ```

    The trade status changes to **"Refund Ready"** (blue badge).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2: Buyer Claims Refund">
    **Who:** The buyer (the wallet that created the escrow)

    **How:** Once the seller has approved, the buyer clicks **"Withdraw Refund"** on the trade detail page.

    **What happens on-chain:**
    ```
    buyerClaimRefund(tradeId)
    ├── Verifies caller is the buyer
    ├── Verifies sellerApprovedRefund == true
    ├── Transfers trade.amount MON back to buyer
    ├── Sets trade.released = true
    └── Emits FundsReleased(id, buyer) event
    ```

    The locked MON is returned to the buyer's wallet, and the trade is finalized.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why Two Steps?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Protects Sellers" icon="shield-check">
    Prevents buyers from claiming refunds after receiving goods. The seller must actively consent, meaning they can refuse if they've already delivered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Protects Buyers" icon="user-shield">
    If the seller is honest and hasn't delivered, they can approve the refund. The buyer can then reclaim their MON without needing a centralized authority.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What If the Seller Refuses to Refund?

If a seller refuses to authorize a refund and you (the buyer) believe you've been wronged:

1. **Use the in-trade chat** — navigate to `/trade/{id}/chat` to discuss the issue directly with the seller
2. **Provide evidence** — share screenshots, tracking numbers, or other proof in the chat
3. **Negotiate** — many disputes can be resolved through communication

<Info>
  **Coming soon:** VeriPay is building a **DAO-based arbitration system** where neutral third parties can review evidence and vote on dispute outcomes. This will be available before mainnet launch.
</Info>

## Refund Scenarios

| Scenario | What Happens |
| --- | --- |
| Goods never shipped | Seller should approve refund → Buyer claims MON back |
| Wrong item delivered | Use chat to negotiate → Seller approves partial or full refund |
| Goods damaged in transit | Use chat with evidence → Seller approves refund |
| Buyer changed their mind | Seller may choose to approve or deny — this is their right |
| Seller disappeared | Currently: funds remain locked. Future: DAO arbitration or time-based auto-refund |
