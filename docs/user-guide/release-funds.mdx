## Confirming Delivery

When the seller has delivered the goods or completed the service, the buyer releases the locked funds. This is the **happy path** — the trade worked as intended.

### Who Can Do This?

Only the **buyer** (the wallet address that created the escrow) can release funds. The seller cannot release funds to themselves.

### Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Trade Detail Page">
    From the Dashboard, click on the trade you want to finalize. Or navigate directly to `/trade/{id}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the Trade Details">
    Confirm that:
    - The trade description matches what you agreed on
    - The seller address is correct
    - You've actually received the goods/service
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Confirm Delivery & Release MON'">
    In the **Settlement Actions** panel, click the black/white button labeled **"Confirm Delivery & Release MON"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the Transaction">
    Your wallet will ask you to approve the `releaseToSeller` transaction. This is a standard blockchain transaction — review and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funds Released">
    Once confirmed on-chain:
    - The locked MON is transferred to the seller's wallet
    - The trade status changes to **"Settled"** (green badge)
    - The trade is finalized — no further actions can be taken
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens On-Chain

```
releaseToSeller(tradeId)
├── Verifies caller is the buyer
├── Verifies trade has not already been released
├── Transfers trade.amount MON to trade.seller
├── Sets trade.released = true
└── Emits FundsReleased(id, seller) event
```

<Warning>
  **This action is irreversible.** Once you release funds to the seller, you cannot undo it. Only click "Confirm Delivery" when you have actually received and verified the goods/service.
</Warning>

## Tips for Buyers

- **Inspect goods thoroughly** before releasing funds
- **Test digital deliverables** (download files, check access) before confirming
- **Use the in-trade chat** if you have questions before releasing
- **Take your time** — there's no deadline to release funds. The escrow stays active until you act.
