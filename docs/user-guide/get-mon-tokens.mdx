## Testnet: Free MON via Faucet

VeriPay currently runs on the **Monad Testnet**, where you can get free MON tokens for testing.

### Using the Monad Faucet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Faucet">
    Go to the official Monad Faucet at [faucet.monad.xyz](https://faucet.monad.xyz).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Wallet Address">
    Paste your wallet address (the `0x...` address shown in VeriPay's navigation bar after connecting).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Tokens">
    Click the request button. You'll receive free testnet MON within seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in Your Wallet">
    Check your wallet balance — the MON tokens should appear immediately. You're ready to create escrows!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Testnet MON has **no real monetary value**. It exists purely for testing purposes. You can request tokens from the faucet multiple times.
</Info>

## Mainnet: Coming Soon

### NGN → MON Onramp (Planned Feature)

When VeriPay launches on Monad Mainnet, we will integrate a **local currency onramp** that allows users to purchase MON tokens directly with Nigerian Naira (NGN):

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment methods** | Bank transfer, USSD, mobile money |
| **Currency** | NGN (Nigerian Naira) → MON |
| **Settlement time** | Near-instant |
| **KYC level** | Minimal (phone number verification) |
| **Supported banks** | All major Nigerian banks |

This means users will be able to:

1. Open VeriPay
2. Click "Buy MON"
3. Pay with their Nigerian bank account
4. Receive MON tokens in their wallet within minutes
5. Create an escrow immediately

<Warning>
  The NGN onramp is currently in development and will be available before or at mainnet launch. Follow [@VeriPayHQ](https://x.com/VeriPayHQ) on X for updates.
</Warning>
