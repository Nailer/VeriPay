## Connecting to VeriPay

VeriPay supports **10+ wallet options** including social login for users who don't have a crypto wallet yet.

### Option A: Connect an Existing Wallet (MetaMask, etc.)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit VeriPay">
    Open [veripay.xyz](https://veripay.xyz) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click the **"Connect Wallet"** button in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Wallet">
    A modal will appear showing all supported wallets. Click on your preferred wallet (e.g., **MetaMask**, **WalletConnect**, **Coinbase Wallet**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the Connection">
    Your wallet extension or app will ask you to approve the connection to VeriPay. Click **"Connect"** or **"Approve"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're Connected!">
    Your wallet address will appear in the navigation bar. You're ready to start creating escrows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Option B: Sign In Without a Wallet (Email / Social Login)

Don't have MetaMask or any crypto wallet? **No problem at all.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit VeriPay">
    Open [veripay.xyz](https://veripay.xyz) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click the **"Connect Wallet"** button in the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Social Login Method">
    Instead of selecting a wallet, choose one of:
    - **📧 Email** — Enter your email address
    - **🔵 Google** — Sign in with your Google account
    - **🍎 Apple** — Sign in with your Apple ID
    - **📘 Facebook** — Sign in with Facebook
    - **📱 Phone** — Enter your phone number
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Identity">
    Depending on your choice, you'll receive a verification code via email, SMS, or be redirected to your social provider's login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wallet Created Automatically">
    VeriPay (powered by Thirdweb) will create a secure, **non-custodial wallet** for you automatically. This wallet is:
    - **Yours** — only you control the private keys
    - **Persistent** — it stays connected across sessions
    - **Compatible** — works like any other crypto wallet
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Social login wallets are powered by [Thirdweb's in-app wallet](https://thirdweb.com/wallets/in-app-wallet) technology. Your private keys are encrypted and only accessible to you — VeriPay never has access to your funds.
</Tip>

## Disconnecting Your Wallet

To disconnect:
1. Click on your wallet address / avatar in the navigation bar
2. Click **"Disconnect"** in the dropdown menu
3. Your session is ended — no data is stored

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Connect button is not responding">
    - Ensure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Brave)
    - Try clearing your browser cache and refreshing the page
    - If using MetaMask, make sure the extension is unlocked
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="MetaMask is not showing in the wallet list">
    - Make sure the MetaMask browser extension is installed
    - Try refreshing the page after installing
    - On mobile, use MetaMask's built-in browser instead of Safari/Chrome
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I connected but my address doesn't show">
    - Wait a few seconds for the connection to sync
    - Try refreshing the page
    - If using WalletConnect, ensure your mobile wallet app has approved the connection
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost access to my social login wallet">
    - Sign in again using the same email/Google/phone number
    - Your wallet is tied to your identity, not your browser session
    - All funds and trade history will be intact
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
