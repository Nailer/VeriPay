## Creating an Escrow

When you create an escrow on VeriPay, you're **locking MON tokens in a smart contract** that acts as a neutral third party. The funds stay locked until you (the buyer) confirm that the seller has delivered what was promised.

### Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Create Page">
    Click **"Start Escrow"** on the homepage, or click **"New Escrow"** in the navigation bar. You can also go directly to `/create`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Seller's Wallet Address">
    In the **"Seller Wallet Address"** field, paste the wallet address of the person you're paying. This is the `0x...` address that will receive the funds once you confirm delivery.

    ```
    Example: 0x742d35Cc6634C0532925a3b844Bc9e7595f2bD18
    ```

    <Warning>
      Double-check the seller's address carefully. Once the escrow is created, the recipient cannot be changed. Sending to the wrong address means the funds can only be released to that address.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Amount">
    In the **"Amount to Secure (MON)"** field, enter the amount of MON tokens you want to lock in escrow.

    - Minimum: Any amount above 0
    - The amount must not exceed your wallet balance
    - This is the exact amount the seller will receive upon delivery confirmation
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the Trade">
    In the **"Trade Terms / Waybill Info"** field, describe what you're buying. Be specific — this becomes a permanent on-chain record that both parties can reference.

    **Good examples:**
    - "iPhone 15 Pro Max, 256GB, Blue. Lagos Island delivery within 48 hours."
    - "Logo design project — 3 concepts, 2 revision rounds. Delivery via Google Drive."
    - "10kg bag of rice, Ofada variety. Pickup at Oshodi Market, Shop B12."

    <Tip>
      The more specific your description, the clearer the trade terms are for both parties. This helps prevent disputes.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the Transaction">
    Click **"Fund & Secure Trade"**. Your wallet will prompt you to approve the transaction.

    - If you're using MetaMask, a popup will ask you to confirm
    - If you're on Monad Testnet for the first time, VeriPay will automatically add the network to your wallet
    - The transaction includes the MON amount you specified — this is the escrow deposit
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Confirmation">
    VeriPay will show a loading spinner while the transaction is being confirmed on the Monad blockchain. This typically takes **under 2 seconds**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Success!">
    Once confirmed, you'll see a success modal showing:
    - ✅ **"Funds Secured!"** confirmation
    - The exact amount locked
    - The seller's address
    - Your trade description

    The seller is **automatically notified** in-app that a new escrow has been created for them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens On-Chain

When you click "Fund & Secure Trade," the following happens at the smart contract level:

```
createTrade(sellerAddress, "iPhone 15 Pro Max, Lagos delivery")
└── msg.value: 0.5 MON (your escrow deposit)
    ├── Creates a new Trade struct with a unique ID
    ├── Records buyer (you), seller, amount, and metadata
    ├── Sets released = false, sellerApprovedRefund = false
    ├── Emits TradeCreated(id, buyer, seller, amount) event
    └── Increments nextTradeId counter
```

Your funds are now **held by the smart contract** — not by VeriPay, not by the seller, not by any human. They can only be moved through the contract's defined functions.

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel an escrow after creating it?">
    You cannot unilaterally cancel an escrow. This is by design — it protects the seller from buyers who might cancel after the seller has already shipped goods. To get your funds back, you need to use the [refund process](/user-guide/request-refund), which requires seller consent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I enter the wrong seller address?">
    Unfortunately, once the escrow is created, the seller address cannot be changed. Always double-check the address before submitting. If you sent to the wrong address, you'll need to coordinate with the actual address owner to have them approve a refund.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much are the gas fees?">
    Gas fees on Monad are extremely low — typically less than $0.01. You'll see the exact fee in your wallet's transaction approval popup before confirming.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a maximum escrow amount?">
    There is no maximum amount in the smart contract. However, on testnet, you're limited by how many MON tokens you can get from the faucet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
