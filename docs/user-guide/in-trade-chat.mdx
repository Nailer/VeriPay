## Trade Messaging

Every active escrow on VeriPay includes a **built-in chat** where the buyer and seller can communicate directly. This is essential for:

- Coordinating delivery details (address, time, method)
- Sharing tracking information or proof of delivery
- Negotiating terms or resolving disputes
- Asking questions before releasing funds or approving refunds

## How to Access Chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Trade Detail Page">
    From the Dashboard, click on the trade you want to discuss.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Resolve Issues'">
    In the Settlement Actions panel, click the **"Resolve Issues"** button. This takes you to the chat page at `/trade/{id}/chat`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Messaging">
    Type your message and send. Both the buyer and seller can see all messages in the thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Chat Features

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Real-time messaging** | Messages appear instantly for both parties |
| **Trade-linked** | Every chat is tied to a specific trade ID |
| **Notification alerts** | The other party receives a notification when you send a message |
| **Wallet-verified** | Messages are associated with your connected wallet address |
| **No personal info needed** | You don't need to share your phone number or email |

## Best Practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Be Specific" icon="bullseye">
    Reference the trade description and be clear about what you need. "The laptop you sent has a cracked screen" is better than "there's a problem."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share Evidence" icon="camera">
    If there's a dispute, describe evidence clearly (photos, tracking numbers, timestamps). Future versions will support image uploads.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stay Professional" icon="handshake">
    Remember that this is a business transaction. Clear, respectful communication resolves most disputes faster than arguing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't Share Private Keys" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Never share your wallet seed phrase, private keys, or passwords in the chat. VeriPay will never ask for these.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
