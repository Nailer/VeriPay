## The Problem

Every day across Africa, millions of peer-to-peer transactions happen through WhatsApp, Instagram, and marketplace platforms. **The trust problem is massive:**

- A buyer sends ₦500,000 for a laptop and receives an empty box — or nothing at all
- A seller ships goods worth ₦200,000 and the buyer disappears without paying
- Traditional escrow services charge 5–10% fees, require paperwork, and take days to settle

**The result?** Over **$50 billion** in informal P2P commerce across Africa operates on pure trust — and when trust fails, people lose everything.

## The Solution

**VeriPay** is a decentralized escrow protocol built on the [Monad blockchain](https://monad.xyz) that eliminates the need for trust between strangers.

Here's how it works in 30 seconds:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buyer locks funds">
    The buyer creates an escrow by depositing MON tokens into VeriPay's smart contract. The funds are locked on-chain — no human can touch them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller delivers">
    The seller sees the locked funds (proof of payment) and delivers the goods or service with confidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buyer confirms">
    Once the buyer receives and verifies delivery, they click **"Confirm Delivery"** to release the funds to the seller.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement in seconds">
    Funds are transferred to the seller's wallet instantly. The trade is finalized on-chain — permanent, transparent, and verifiable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If something goes wrong, VeriPay has a built-in **refund flow** and **in-trade chat** for dispute resolution. The seller must consent to a refund — preventing abuse from either side.
</Note>
