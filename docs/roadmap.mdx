## 🔄 v1.5 — Enhanced Features (In Development)

**ETA: Q3–Q4 2026**

<Steps>
  <Step title="NGN → MON Onramp">
    Local currency conversion allowing Nigerian users to purchase MON tokens directly with Naira via bank transfer, USSD, or mobile money. **This is the single most important feature for mass adoption in Africa.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade History & Receipt Export">
    Persistent trade history per wallet address with the ability to export receipts as PDF documents for record-keeping and accounting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller Reputation System">
    On-chain reputation scores based on completed trades, response time, and refund history. Helps buyers identify trustworthy sellers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Multi-Language Support">
    Interface translations for major Nigerian languages including Yoruba, Pidgin English, Hausa, and Igbo — making VeriPay accessible to non-English speakers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enhanced Dispute Resolution">
    Image/file uploads in trade chat, structured dispute forms, and escalation workflows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 🔮 v2.0 — Mainnet & Scale (Future Vision)

**ETA: 2027**

| Feature | Description | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monad Mainnet Deployment** | Move from testnet to mainnet — real money, real trades | Production-ready |
| **Multi-Token Escrow** | Support USDC, USDT, and custom ERC-20 tokens | Stablecoin adoption |
| **Professional Audit** | Third-party security audit by a top firm | Institutional trust |
| **Mobile App** | Native iOS/Android app (React Native) | Wider reach |
| **Marketplace Integrations** | Plugin for Jiji, Jumia, and social commerce platforms | Distribution |
| **DAO Governance** | Decentralized dispute arbitration by community voters | Trustless resolution |
| **Cross-Border Escrow** | Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, and pan-African support | Continental scale |
| **Merchant SDK** | JavaScript SDK for merchants to embed VeriPay in their stores | B2B channel |
| **Time-Locked Escrow** | Auto-refund if seller doesn't deliver within a deadline | Buyer protection |
| **Multi-Sig Escrow** | 2-of-3 or 3-of-5 escrow for high-value trades | Enterprise use |
