## The Origin Story

VeriPay was born from a simple frustration: **why is it still so risky to buy and sell things online in Lagos?**

In Nigeria and across Africa, peer-to-peer commerce is the backbone of the economy. People buy phones on WhatsApp. Freelancers get hired through Twitter DMs. Vendors sell on Instagram. But every single one of these transactions requires a leap of faith — you either pay first and hope the seller delivers, or you deliver first and hope the buyer pays.

**Too often, that trust is betrayed.** Scammers run fake vendor pages. Buyers claim they never received goods. And the existing "solutions" — traditional escrow companies, bank guarantees, or "pay on delivery" services — are slow, expensive, and riddled with their own trust issues.

## The Mission

> **To make every peer-to-peer transaction in Africa as safe as buying from a Fortune 500 company — but without the middlemen, fees, or waiting.**

VeriPay achieves this by replacing human trust with **mathematical certainty**. Our smart contract is the escrow agent — it holds funds exactly as programmed, releases them exactly when conditions are met, and cannot be bribed, hacked through social engineering, or persuaded to play favorites.
