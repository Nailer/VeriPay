## Core Features

VeriPay is packed with features designed to make decentralized escrow accessible, secure, and delightful to use.

### ⚡ Lightning-Fast Settlement

Powered by Monad's parallel execution engine, VeriPay transactions settle in **under 2 seconds**. Compare that to:

| Platform | Settlement Time |
| --- | --- |
| **VeriPay (Monad)** | **< 2 seconds** |
| Ethereum L1 | 12–15 seconds |
| Traditional escrow | 3–7 business days |
| Bank wire transfer | 1–3 business days |

### 🔒 Trustless Smart Contract Escrow

Funds are locked in an **immutable, permissionless smart contract** on the Monad blockchain. This means:

- **No human can access your funds** — not VeriPay, not a bank, not anyone
- **Funds are released only when you confirm** delivery of goods/services
- **The contract has no admin keys** — there is no "owner" who can drain funds or change rules
- **Every action is verifiable on-chain** via the [Monad block explorer](https://monad-testnet.socialscan.io/address/0xd0cc532f55ce6849d5b70e24d6188073f8921621)

### 🌍 Built for Africa

VeriPay is designed from the ground up for the African market:

- **Lagos-first UX** — built for how Nigerians actually trade (social media, WhatsApp deals, marketplace transactions)
- **Low-bandwidth optimized** — works on slow connections and budget smartphones
- **Local context** — trade descriptions like "iPhone 15 Pro Max, Lagos delivery within 48 hours" are first-class citizens
- **Coming soon:** NGN → MON onramp so users can fund escrows directly with Naira

### 💬 In-Trade Messaging

Every active escrow has a **built-in chat** where buyer and seller can communicate directly:

- Discuss trade details, delivery timelines, and conditions
- Resolve disputes without leaving the platform
- Messages are linked to the specific trade ID for context
- No need to share phone numbers or personal contact info

### 🧾 Consent-Based Refund System

VeriPay's refund mechanism is designed to be **fair to both parties**:

1. **Buyer requests refund** — indicates a problem with the trade
2. **Seller must approve** — the seller clicks "Authorize Return of Funds" to consent
3. **Buyer claims refund** — only after seller approval, the buyer can withdraw their MON

This two-step consent model prevents:
- Buyers from fraudulently claiming refunds after receiving goods
- One-sided disputes where the seller has no say

<Warning>
  If a seller refuses to approve a refund and the buyer believes they've been scammed, they should use the **in-trade chat** to negotiate. Future versions will include **DAO-based arbitration** for unresolved disputes.
</Warning>
