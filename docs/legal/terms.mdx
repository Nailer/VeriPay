## 1. What VeriPay is

VeriPay is experimental software that lets buyers lock payments in a smart contract on Monad **testnet** until delivery. During the testnet phase no real money is moved: card payments run in Paystack test mode and on-chain naira (vNGN) is a test token with no value.

## 2. Non-custodial service

Funds locked in an order are held by the `VeriPayEscrow` smart contract, not by VeriPay. VeriPay cannot move money held for open orders. Outcomes are determined by the contract's rules: buyer confirmation, seller-approved refund, arbitration of a reported problem, or automatic release after the confirmation window.

## 3. Your responsibilities

- Keep your sign-in method (passkey, wallet) secure; VeriPay cannot recover lost accounts.
- Only confirm delivery once you have received what you paid for — confirmation is irreversible.
- Report problems before the confirmation window ends.
- Describe items accurately and keep order communication in the order chat.
- Don't use VeriPay for illegal goods or services, fraud, or to evade law.

## 4. Fees

A fee (currently 1%, never more than 5%) is deducted from the seller's payout when the seller is paid. Refunds carry no fee. Network fees may be sponsored by VeriPay at its discretion.

## 5. Disputes

If a problem is reported, an arbitrator designated in the contract may split the locked amount between buyer and seller. The arbitrator's on-chain decision is final as far as the contract is concerned.

## 6. No warranty
