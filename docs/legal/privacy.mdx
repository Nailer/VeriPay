## Overview

VeriPay is designed with **privacy by default**. We collect as little data as possible and are transparent about what we do collect.

## What We Collect

### On-Chain Data (Public by Design)

The following data is **permanently recorded on the Monad blockchain** and is publicly accessible to anyone:

- Buyer wallet addresses
- Seller wallet addresses
- Escrow amounts (in MON)
- Trade descriptions (metadata)
- Transaction hashes and timestamps

This data is on-chain by necessity — it's how the escrow protocol works. By creating an escrow, you consent to this data being publicly visible on the blockchain.

### Off-Chain Data (VeriPay Servers)

| Data Type | What We Store | Why | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notifications | Wallet address, trade ID, notification type, read status | To deliver trade alerts | Until user clears notifications |
| Chat messages | Trade ID, sender address, message text, timestamp | To enable in-trade communication | Until trade is deleted |

### What We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ Real names or personal identification
- ❌ Email addresses (unless you choose social login, handled by Thirdweb)
- ❌ Phone numbers (unless you choose phone login, handled by Thirdweb)
- ❌ IP addresses (stored by hosting infrastructure only, not VeriPay)
- ❌ Browser fingerprints or tracking cookies
- ❌ Financial data (your private keys, seed phrases, bank details)

## Social Login (Thirdweb)

If you choose to sign in with email, Google, Apple, Facebook, or phone, these credentials are handled by [Thirdweb](https://thirdweb.com/privacy-policy) — not VeriPay directly. VeriPay only receives your generated wallet address. Please review [Thirdweb's Privacy Policy](https://thirdweb.com/privacy-policy) for details on how they handle your social login data.

## How We Use Data

- **Notifications:** To alert you when trade events occur involving your wallet
- **Chat:** To enable communication between trade participants
- We do **not** sell, share, or monetize your data in any way

## Your Rights

- **Access:** Request a copy of any data we hold about your wallet address
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your off-chain data (notifications, chat messages)
- **Portability:** All on-chain data is already publicly accessible via the block explorer

## Data Security

- Off-chain data is stored with industry-standard security practices
- We do not store private keys, seed phrases, or sensitive financial information
- HTTPS is enforced for all connections to VeriPay's servers

For privacy-related requests, contact us at [privacy@veripay.xyz](mailto:privacy@veripay.xyz).
