## Who is VeriPay For?

VeriPay is designed for anyone who buys or sells peer-to-peer and needs a trustworthy way to ensure both sides keep their promises.

---

## 🛒 P2P Marketplace Transactions

**The most common use case — buying and selling on social media and marketplaces.**

### The Problem
You find an iPhone for sale on WhatsApp or Instagram. The seller wants payment before shipping. You don't know if they're legit. They don't know if you'll pay after delivery.

### With VeriPay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buyer creates escrow">
    "iPhone 15 Pro Max, 256GB, Blue Titanium. Lagos Island delivery within 48 hours. ₦0.5 MON."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller sees locked funds">
    The seller checks VeriPay and sees the MON is locked — proof the buyer has the money and is serious.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller ships the item">
    Confident that payment is secured, the seller ships the iPhone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buyer receives and confirms">
    Buyer inspects the iPhone, confirms it matches the description, and releases funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller receives payment">
    MON is instantly transferred to the seller's wallet. Trade complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Perfect For
- Electronics (phones, laptops, gaming consoles)
- Fashion and clothing
- Car parts and accessories
- Household items
- Any physical goods traded on WhatsApp, Instagram, Twitter/X, or Facebook Marketplace

## 💻 Freelancer Payments

**Protecting both clients and freelancers in service-based work.**

### The Problem
A client hires a graphic designer for a logo project. The freelancer is worried about doing the work and not getting paid. The client is worried about paying upfront and getting low-quality work.

### With VeriPay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Client creates escrow">
    "Logo design — 3 initial concepts, 2 revision rounds. Final files: AI, PNG, SVG. 0.3 MON."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Freelancer begins work">
    Seeing the locked funds, the freelancer starts the project with confidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Freelancer delivers via chat">
    Uses VeriPay's in-trade chat to share progress updates and final files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client approves and releases">
    Client reviews the deliverables, approves quality, and releases payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Perfect For
- Graphic design and branding
- Web development projects
- Content writing and copywriting
- Video editing
- Virtual assistant services
- Translation services

## 🏠 Real Estate Deposits

**Securing rental deposits and property transaction earnest money.**

### The Problem
A tenant wants to rent an apartment. The landlord demands a deposit. The tenant worries the landlord will keep the deposit unfairly. The landlord worries the tenant will damage the property.

### With VeriPay

- Tenant locks the deposit in VeriPay
- At the end of the lease, if the property is in good condition, the landlord releases the deposit back to the tenant
- If there's damage, they negotiate via in-trade chat
- The deposit is transparent and verifiable — no "I never received it" disputes

## 🌍 Cross-Border Remittances (Future)

**Sending money across borders with escrow protection.**

### The Vision
A Nigerian living in the UK wants to send money home for a specific purpose (e.g., paying a contractor to renovate a family house). With VeriPay:

1. Sender creates an escrow specifying the contractor's address and the work scope
2. Contractor sees the locked funds and begins work
3. Sender (or a designated verifier) confirms completion
4. Funds release to the contractor

This adds **accountability** to remittances — ensuring the money is used for its intended purpose.

## 🏢 B2B Trade Settlement

**Business-to-business wholesale and supply chain transactions.**

### The Problem
A restaurant needs to order supplies from a wholesale vendor. They've been burned before by vendors who take payment and deliver short. The vendor has been burned by restaurants that receive goods and delay payment for months.

### With VeriPay
- Restaurant locks payment for the supply order
- Vendor delivers the full order
- Restaurant confirms receipt and releases payment
- Both sides are protected, building long-term trust
