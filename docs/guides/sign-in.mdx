## The short version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the fingerprint button">
    On any page that needs an account (paying, selling, your orders), tap **Use Face ID / fingerprint**, or the fingerprint icon next to **Sign in** in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve on your device">
    Your phone or laptop asks for your face, fingerprint or device PIN. Approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    The top bar now shows **Signed in** (or your shop name if you're a seller). There's nothing to write down and no password to remember.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's actually happening

The first time, the browser registers a **WebAuthn passkey** for `veripay.store` with the **PRF extension** enabled. On every sign-in the authenticator evaluates the PRF and returns the same 32-byte output. VeriPay's page — on your device — converts that output into an Ethereum-compatible key (BIP-39 entropy → seed → BIP-32 path `m/44'/60'/0'/0/0`) and uses it to sign your transactions.

<Accordion title="In plain words" icon="lightbulb">
  Your device keeps a special secret locked behind your fingerprint. Each time you sign in, it produces the same "answer", and VeriPay's page turns that answer into your account — right there on your phone. VeriPay's servers never see the secret.
</Accordion>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Same account everywhere your passkey is" icon="refresh-cw">
    If your passkey syncs (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager), signing in on another device gives you the **same** account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bound to veripay.store" icon="globe">
    Passkeys are tied to the website's domain. An account created on a different VeriPay address (for example, a preview URL) is a different account.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Supported devices

| Platform | Works with | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone / iPad | Face ID, Touch ID | Needs a recent iOS (18 or later) for PRF support |
| Mac | Touch ID, iCloud Keychain | Safari, Chrome |
| Android | Fingerprint, face unlock, screen lock | Chrome with Google Password Manager |
| Windows | Windows Hello | Chrome / Edge |
| Security keys | FIDO2 keys with `hmac-secret` | Most modern YubiKeys |

<Accordion title="Why some devices don't work">
  VeriPay needs the WebAuthn **PRF extension**, which turns a passkey into a stable secret. Older browsers and some password managers don't support it. When that happens, Mera returns `PRF_UNAVAILABLE` and you'll see: *"This device's biometric hardware doesn't support the passkey feature we need. Try Sign in instead."*

  **In plain words:** some older phones or browsers can't do this trick. Use the other sign-in options instead.
</Accordion>

## Other ways to sign in

If fingerprint sign-in isn't available, tap **Other ways to sign in** (or **Sign in**). You can use:

- **Email**, **phone**, **Google**, **Apple** or **Facebook** (thirdweb in-app wallet)
- **MetaMask**, **Coinbase Wallet**, **Rainbow**, **Rabby**, **Zerion**
- **WalletConnect** (any mobile wallet)

<Note>
  Each method gives you a **different account**. If you signed up as a seller with your fingerprint, keep using your fingerprint so you see your orders and your link.
</Note>

## Signing out and switching

Open the account menu in the top bar and choose **Disconnect**. Signing in again with the same method returns the same account.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Cancelled, or your device couldn't complete Face ID / fingerprint verification.&quot;">
    You dismissed the prompt or the biometric check failed. Try again; if it keeps failing, check your device's biometrics settings. (Mera error code `PASSKEY_OPERATION_FAILED`.)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I signed in but I see different orders than before">
    You're probably using a different method or a different passkey than last time. Sign out and sign in the way you did originally.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost my phone">
    If your passkey was synced to your cloud account, sign in on a new device with the same cloud account and the passkey will be there. If it wasn't synced, the account cannot be recovered — this is why synced passkeys are recommended.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
