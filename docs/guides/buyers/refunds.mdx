A refund is a **two-step, consent-based** flow: the seller approves, then the buyer claims. Refunds always return **100%** of the amount with **no fee**.

<Accordion title="In plain words" icon="lightbulb">
  The seller has to agree to give the money back, and then you collect it. You always get every naira back — VeriPay takes nothing.
</Accordion>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the seller">
    Message them in the [order chat](/guides/order-chat) and ask for a refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller approves">
    The seller taps **Refund the buyer** on the order page. The order shows **Refund ready**: *"The seller agreed to a refund. Take it back whenever you're ready."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="You claim it">
    Tap **Take my refund**. The full amount returns to your account and the order shows **Refunded**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Behind the buttons

| Step | Call | Checks | Event |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seller approves | `sellerApproveRefund(id)` | caller is the seller; order open | `RefundAuthorized(id)` |
| Buyer claims | `buyerClaimRefund(id)` | caller is the buyer; order open; `sellerApprovedRefund == true` | `FundsReleased(id, buyer)` |

`_payout(id, amount, 0)` sends the whole amount to the buyer. Because `toSeller == 0`, no fee is computed.

<Accordion title="Why two steps instead of one?">
  The seller can't push money to the buyer and the buyer can't pull it without consent. Splitting it means a seller can't be forced into a refund, and the buyer stays in control of when it happens. If the seller refuses, the buyer's remedy is [Report a problem](/guides/buyers/report-a-problem).

  **In plain words:** the seller says "yes, refund", and you decide when to collect. If the seller won't agree, you can ask for a review instead.
</Accordion>

## Where does the refunded money go?

Back to the account you paid from, as the same asset (vNGN on pay links). On testnet it can't be withdrawn to a bank. On mainnet, the plan is a regulated naira stablecoin with a cash-out partner — see the [Roadmap](/resources/roadmap).

## Record effects

Your record gets **+1 Refunds** (as a buyer). The seller's **Paid out** does not change.
