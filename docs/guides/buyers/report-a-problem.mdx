## When to use it

Use **Report a problem** when talking it out hasn't worked:

- The item never arrived and the seller stopped replying.
- You received something different, fake or broken.
- The seller won't refund but you believe you're owed one.

<Tip>
  Try the [order chat](/guides/order-chat) first. If the seller agrees to refund, they can do it directly and you get 100% back faster — see [Refunds](/guides/buyers/refunds).
</Tip>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    **My orders** → the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Report a problem with this order">
    At the bottom of the action panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Read the warning — *"This freezes the money. Neither of you can release or refund it until a VeriPay agent reviews the order and decides who gets what."* — and tap **Freeze & request review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain in the chat">
    A system message is posted to the order chat so an agent sees it in the admin console. Add your evidence: photos, tracking numbers, what was agreed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the decision">
    The order shows **Under review** and the money is **Frozen**. The reviewer decides a split; you'll see **Settled by review**, **Refunded** or **Completed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Report before the confirmation window ends.** After the 7-day window, the order becomes eligible for automatic payout to the seller. Once paid out, it can't be disputed.
</Warning>

## Behind the button

`raiseDispute(id)` may be called once, by the buyer or the seller, on an open order. It sets `disputed = true` and emits `DisputeRaised(id, by)`. While `disputed` is true:

- `autoRelease(id)` reverts with `trade is disputed` — the seller **cannot** be paid automatically.
- Only the arbitrator can settle it with `resolveDispute(id, buyerBps)`, choosing any split from 0 to 10,000 basis points.
- The buyer can still release voluntarily; a seller-approved refund can still be claimed.

<Accordion title="In plain words" icon="lightbulb">
  Reporting a problem puts the money in a "frozen" state. The automatic payout stops. Only the VeriPay reviewer can unfreeze it by deciding how much goes to you and how much to the seller — anything from all to you, to all to the seller, or a split in between. You can still choose to pay the seller yourself if things get sorted out.
</Accordion>

## How the reviewer decides

| Ruling | `buyerBps` | You receive | Seller receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full refund | `10000` | 100% | 0 |
| Split down the middle | `5000` | 50% | 50% minus 1% fee on their half |
| Seller in the right | `0` | 0 | 100% minus 1% fee |

The fee is only ever charged on the seller's portion — a full refund costs you nothing. See [Disputes and arbitration](/architecture/disputes-and-arbitration).

## Effect on records

A dispute adds **+1 Disputes** to **both** your record and the seller's, regardless of the outcome. This discourages frivolous reports from either side.
