Open **My orders** in the top bar (or [veripay.store/orders](https://veripay.store/orders)) and sign in. You'll see every order where your account is the buyer or the seller, newest first.

## Status labels

| Label | You are | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Held safely — confirm when it arrives** | Buyer | Your money is locked | Confirm when the order arrives, or report a problem |
| **Paid — deliver this order** | Seller | The buyer's money is locked for you | Deliver |
| **Under review** | Either | A problem was reported; money frozen | Add evidence in the chat; wait for the decision |
| **Refunded to you** | Buyer | You were repaid in full | Nothing |
| **Refunded to buyer** | Seller | The buyer was repaid | Nothing |
| **Settled by review** | Either | A reviewer split the money | Nothing |
| **Completed** | Buyer | The seller was paid | Nothing |
| **Paid out to you** | Seller | You were paid | Nothing |

Orders needing your action are highlighted.

<Accordion title="Where does this list come from?" icon="database">
  `OrdersList` calls `GET /api/reputation/<your address>?trades=1`, which always reads your trades **directly from the escrow contract** (the newest 1,000 orders, cached 30 s) and returns a `TradeSummary` per order: id, your role, counterparty, amount and symbol (formatted by asset — `₦` for vNGN), status (`open | released | refunded | disputed | split`) and description. No database is involved in deciding status.

  **In plain words:** your order list is read straight from the blockchain, so it always matches reality.
</Accordion>

## The order page

Tapping an order opens `/trade/<id>`:

- **Amount** and what the seller receives after the fee.
- **Status**: *Held safely*, *Frozen*, *Paid out* or *Refunded*.
- **Countdown** to automatic release, e.g. *"2d 5h left for the buyer to confirm delivery."*
- **Buyer** and **Seller** (shop name and record badge for sellers with a link).
- **Actions** for whichever role you are.
- **Message the seller / buyer** to open the chat.

Visitors who aren't the buyer or seller can view an order but see *"You're viewing this order as a visitor — only its buyer and seller can act on it."*
