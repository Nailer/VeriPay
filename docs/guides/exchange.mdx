**Buy / sell crypto** (`/exchange`, under **More**) is a naira on-ramp and off-ramp built before the hackathon. It exists for one reason: using escrow directly in MON required owning MON, and almost nobody in Nigeria does. **The product is the escrow; the exchange is a feature.**

<Accordion title="In plain words" icon="lightbulb">
  This lets you swap naira for crypto and back. Most people won't need it — paying through a seller's link already handles naira for you.
</Accordion>

## Supported coins

MON, USDT, BTC, ETH, SOL. Automatic delivery from the hot wallet is supported for **MON**; other coins are settled manually by an admin.

## Quotes

- Prices are computed **on the server** from live markets: coin → USD (Binance, CoinGecko fallback) × USD → NGN (median of the live **Bybit P2P** USDT/NGN book).
- A **3.5% spread** is applied (buy above mid, sell below).
- Your quote is **locked for 15 minutes** with a live countdown. Unpaid orders then **expire** — the only automatic status change, and it only moves an order backwards.
- Limits: **₦1,000 – ₦5,000,000** per order (configurable).

See [Exchange engine](/architecture/exchange-engine) for the full pricing model.

## Buy crypto

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="By bank transfer">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create an order">Choose a coin, enter naira, paste the wallet address to receive the crypto.</Step>
      <Step title="Transfer the exact amount">
        You'll be shown an **exact** amount with a unique kobo suffix — e.g. **₦20,000.14**, not ₦20,000 — plus bank details and a reference. The suffix lets the admin match your transfer in the bank statement.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Declare payment">Tap that you've paid and enter the **name on the account you paid from**. Status → `payment_review`.</Step>
      <Step title="Admin confirms">An admin matches the credit and confirms; crypto is sent. Status → `completed` (or `rejected` with a reason).</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By card">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create an order">Choose **card**. Paystack's fee (1.5% + ₦100 above ₦2,500, capped ₦2,000) is shown as a separate line.</Step>
      <Step title="Pay in the popup">Paystack charges the exact amount.</Step>
      <Step title="Automatic verification">The server verifies with Paystack; status → `verified`. If the hot wallet is configured, MON is sent immediately → `completed`. A signed Paystack **webhook** settles the order even if you close the tab.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sell crypto

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a sell order">Choose the coin and amount, and enter your bank, 10-digit account number and account name.</Step>
  <Step title="Send the crypto">Send exactly the quoted amount from the wallet you entered to VeriPay's deposit address.</Step>
  <Step title="Submit the transaction hash">
    The server reads the transaction from a Monad node and checks it **exists, succeeded, went to VeriPay's address, came from your wallet, and carries enough value**. A unique index on `lower(tx_hash)` stops one deposit settling two orders. Status → `verified`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid in naira">An admin sends the naira to your bank and marks the order paid out → `completed`.</Step>
</Steps>

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `awaiting_payment` | Quote locked; waiting for your payment/deposit |
| `payment_review` | You declared a bank transfer; admin is matching it |
| `verified` | Payment proven (card via Paystack, or deposit on-chain) |
| `completed` | Settled |
| `rejected` | Admin couldn't match the payment |
| `expired` | Quote lapsed before payment |
| `cancelled` | You cancelled an unpaid order |

<Warning>
  If you already sent money and the quote **expired**, don't create a new order — use the support chat on the order page and an agent will sort it out.
</Warning>
