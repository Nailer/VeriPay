## Where to put it

| Place | How |
|---|---|
| **Instagram bio** | Edit profile → Links → add `veripay.store/pay/your-shop` |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Profile → Website; also pin it in your catalogue description |
| **WhatsApp status** | Post the link with a short explanation |
| **Jiji / marketplace listings** | Paste into the listing description and chat |
| **Every DM that asks "how do I pay?"** | Send the link instead of an account number |

## Copy you can paste

<CodeGroup>
```text Instagram bio
🛡️ Pay safely: veripay.store/pay/your-shop
Your money is held until your order arrives.
```

```text WhatsApp reply
Hi! You can pay here: veripay.store/pay/your-shop
You'll see my track record before paying, and your money is held safely until you receive your order — I only get paid after you confirm. 🙏
```

```text Answering "are you legit?"
Check my record here — it's counted from real orders and I can't edit it: veripay.store/pay/your-shop
And your money stays protected until your order arrives.
```
</CodeGroup>

## Tips

- **Pin it.** Make it the first thing new followers see.
- **Mention protection, not crypto.** Customers care that their money is safe — not how.
- **Point sceptics to your record.** A growing *Paid out* count is your strongest sales tool.
- **Put the same link everywhere.** One link builds one record.
