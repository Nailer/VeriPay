## Where buyers see it

- At the top of your **payment link** page, before they pay.
- On every **order page**, under *Seller*.
- At [veripay.store/seller](https://veripay.store/seller) when someone types your link name, and at `/seller/<your address>` with your full order history.
- On **Pay with crypto**, as soon as a buyer pastes your address as the seller.

## How it's counted

| Figure | Increases when… |
|---|---|
| **Total orders** | You're the buyer **or** seller on any new order (+1) |
| **Paid out** | You're the seller and you got paid — buyer confirmed, auto-release, or a ruling gave you any share (+1) |
| **Disputes** | Anyone reported a problem on an order you're part of (+1 to **both** sides) |
| **Refunds** | You're the **buyer** and were repaid — refund claim or a ruling that gave you any share (+1) |

These rules are implemented twice — in the Envio indexer (`indexer/src/EventHandlers.ts`) and in the chain fallback (`src/lib/reputation.ts`) — and verified to produce identical numbers for every address that has traded.

<Accordion title="In plain words" icon="lightbulb">
  Every order you finish successfully adds one to "Paid out". Every time someone reports a problem on your order, "Disputes" goes up for both of you. The numbers are counted from public records, so they're always the same no matter who counts them.
</Accordion>

## What you can't do

- **Edit or delete** an order from your history. The events are permanent.
- **Import** reviews from elsewhere.
- **Pay yourself** to inflate your record — your own link refuses your account, and the contract rejects `buyer == seller`.

## Growing your record honestly

1. Route every sale through your link, even repeat customers.
2. Deliver fast and ask happy buyers to tap **I received my order**.
3. Refund quickly when you can't deliver, instead of letting it become a dispute.
4. Keep conversations in the order chat so disputes, if any, resolve in your favour when you're right.
