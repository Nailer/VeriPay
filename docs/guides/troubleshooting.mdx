## Sign-in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The fingerprint button doesn't appear">
    Your browser doesn't support WebAuthn. Update it, or use **Sign in** with email, Google, Apple or a wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;This device's biometric hardware doesn't support the passkey feature we need.&quot;">
    Your device or password manager doesn't support the WebAuthn PRF extension. Use **Other ways to sign in**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My orders / link disappeared after signing in">
    You're on a different account — likely a different sign-in method or a different passkey. Sign out and use the original method.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Paying

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I was charged but there's no receipt">
    Reopen the seller's link, sign in the same way and enter the same amount. The page detects your payment and finishes locking it **without charging again**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Your account isn't quite ready yet. Wait a few seconds and try again.&quot;">
    Your network-fee top-up is still landing. Wait ~5 seconds and retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;That payment reference doesn't belong to this payment.&quot;">
    The card charge was for a different order. Start again from the seller's link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;The minimum is ₦100.&quot; / &quot;The maximum is ₦5,000,000.&quot;">
    Pay-link payments must be between ₦100 and ₦5,000,000.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Say what you're buying, so you and the seller agree on it.&quot;">
    The item description needs at least 3 characters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Transaction cancelled.&quot;">
    You (or your wallet) declined the signature. Try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## After paying

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The seller says they didn't get a notification">
    They may not have enabled device alerts. Ask them to check **My orders** or the **Sell** page — every paid order is listed there, read from the chain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The countdown is about to run out and my order hasn't arrived">
    Report a problem **now**. Once the window passes and nobody has reported a problem, the seller can be paid automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The order page says &quot;We couldn't load this order.&quot;">
    A network read failed. Check your connection and refresh.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see buttons on the order">
    You're not signed in as the buyer or seller of that order. Visitors can only view.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Notifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Blocked in Browser Settings&quot;">
    You denied notification permission before. Re-allow notifications for `veripay.store` in your browser or phone settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No push on iPhone">
    Install VeriPay to your home screen first, then enable alerts from inside the installed app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
