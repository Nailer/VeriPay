  VeriPay was entered into Monad's Metropolis hackathon in the "consumer products and payments" category, whose whole theme is making blockchain invisible to everyday users — exactly VeriPay's idea.
</Accordion>

## One line

Buyer protection for Instagram and WhatsApp shopping. A seller puts one link in their bio; the buyer taps it, sees the seller's real track record, and pays in naira with a fingerprint. The money is held until the order arrives. The buyer never sees a wallet, a token or a gas fee.

## Pre-existing vs built during Metropolis

VeriPay was started on **11 April 2026**. Everything dated before 1 September 2026 is foundation; everything after is the submission.

## How to evaluate (5 minutes)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Main flow">Follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart): claim a link, pay it from a second browser with the test card, confirm delivery.</Step>
  <Step title="Check a seller">Type `adaeze-thrift` at `/seller`.</Step>
  <Step title="Passkey">Tap the fingerprint icon next to **Sign in**.</Step>
  <Step title="Auto-release">Open the [proof of order #2's Chainlink release](https://veripay.store/proof/0xa5cf52aeac617514c10055bf703c2766eb5366f640958c171006681b9d87f823).</Step>
  <Step title="Tests">`cd contracts/test && npm test` (30) and `node tests/e2e-pay.mjs` (14).</Step>
</Steps>

## Judging criteria mapping

| Criterion (20% each) | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Product quality | Plain-language consumer flow; receipts; proof page; PWA |
| Technical excellence | Server-verified money path, CAS guards, event-sourced reputation with parity fallback, tests |
| Monad integration | ~1 s locks, < ₦1 fees sponsored, event per order — [Why Monad](/product/why-monad) |
| Track fit | No wallet, token, seed phrase or gas fee visible to users |
| Innovation & impact | Unfakeable seller records for social commerce; escrow for ₦2,000 orders |
