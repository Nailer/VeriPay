### Brand Colors

| Name | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VeriPay Pink | `#FF007A` | Primary brand color, CTAs |
| VeriPay Purple | `#9945FF` | Gradient accent |
| Dark Background | `#09090B` | Dark mode background |
| Light Background | `#FFFFFF` | Light mode background |

### Logo Usage Guidelines

- Always use the official VeriPay logo on a contrasting background
- Do not modify, distort, or recolor the logo without permission
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "V" letterform
- Do not use the logo to imply endorsement without written permission
