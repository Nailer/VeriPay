<Warning>
  VeriPay is experimental software running on a test network. Nothing here is financial, legal or investment advice.
</Warning>

## For users

| Risk | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Testnet** | Balances are test tokens with no value; they can't be withdrawn to a bank. |
| **Smart-contract risk** | Unaudited code may contain bugs that lock or misdirect funds. |
| **Deadline risk** | If you don't confirm or report a problem within the window (default 7 days), the seller can be paid automatically. |
| **Irreversibility** | Confirming delivery pays the seller immediately and can't be undone. |
| **Arbitration risk** | Disputes are decided by a single reviewer whose decision is final on-chain. |
| **Account recovery** | If your passkey isn't synced and you lose the device, the account can't be recovered. |
| **Reputation is evidence, not a guarantee** | Good records can be gamed or a seller can change behaviour. |
| **Third-party dependencies** | Paystack, Supabase, Alchemy, Envio and Chainlink outages can delay actions. |

<Accordion title="In plain words" icon="lightbulb">
  It's a test version. The money isn't real yet. Watch the 7-day deadline, only confirm when you have your item, and remember a good seller record is helpful but not a promise.
</Accordion>

## For integrators

- Interfaces and addresses may change before mainnet.
- Envio dev-tier endpoints may move.
- Public API routes are not versioned and have no SLA.

## For investors and partners

- No real users or revenue yet; all on-chain activity is the team's own testing.
- Regulatory: escrow and payments in Nigeria may require licensing; a naira stablecoin and bank payouts require licensed partners.
- Key-person risk: a team of one.
- Market risk: adoption by social-commerce sellers is unproven.
