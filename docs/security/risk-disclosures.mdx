## Important Disclosures

<Warning>
  Please read these disclosures carefully before using VeriPay. Understanding the risks helps you make informed decisions about using the platform.
</Warning>

### Testnet Disclaimer

VeriPay is currently deployed on the **Monad Testnet**. This means:

- MON tokens on testnet have **no real monetary value**
- The testnet may experience downtime, resets, or data wipes
- Features and UI may change significantly before mainnet launch
- Do not use VeriPay for real financial transactions until mainnet launch is announced

### Smart Contract Risk

While VeriPay's smart contract is designed to be secure:

- **No smart contract is 100% bug-free** — undiscovered vulnerabilities may exist
- The contract has not yet been audited by a professional third-party firm
- Once funds are locked in the contract, they can only be moved through the contract's defined functions
- If a bug is discovered after mainnet launch, the contract **cannot be patched** (it is immutable)

### Blockchain Risk

Using any blockchain-based application involves inherent risks:

- **Transaction irreversibility** — blockchain transactions cannot be undone
- **Private key responsibility** — if you lose your private keys, your funds are permanently lost
- **Network risk** — the Monad network could experience outages or consensus failures
- **Gas price volatility** — gas fees may increase during periods of high network activity

### Regulatory Risk

- Cryptocurrency regulations vary by jurisdiction and are evolving rapidly
- VeriPay does not provide legal, financial, or tax advice
- Users are responsible for complying with local laws regarding cryptocurrency use
- The regulatory status of escrow services on blockchain is uncertain in many jurisdictions

### Not Financial Advice

VeriPay is a technology platform, not a financial institution. We do not:

- Provide investment advice
- Guarantee the value of any cryptocurrency
- Insure deposits or transactions
- Act as a regulated escrow agent
